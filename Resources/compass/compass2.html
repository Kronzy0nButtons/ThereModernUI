Appearance
Accent colour for the whole UI, and what the dock shows besides its buttons.
Controls
In Action Mode (or while aiming a gun): W A S D move, Space jumps, R reloads and other letters don’t type. Press Enter to chat (Action Chat) and Enter again to send. F1 to F12 use your Quick Bar slots at any time.
UI Behaviour
How the dock behaves while you play: panels that close themselves after you use them, and the speedometer that appears when you drive.

About
A replacement for the game’s original Compass, Communicator and Message Bar. It also brings ChangeMe’s body and face editing into the Wardrobe, puts the volume sliders in the dock, gives the trade window a matching look, and ships rebuilt There Edge components that make the client noticeably smoother while its windows load.
