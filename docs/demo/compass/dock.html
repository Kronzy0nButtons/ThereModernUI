Appearance
Accent colour for the whole UI, and what the dock shows besides its buttons.
Controls
In Action Mode (or while aiming a gun): W A S D move, Space jumps, R reloads and other letters don’t type. Press Enter to chat (Action Chat) and Enter again to send. F1 to F12 use your Quick Bar slots at any time.
GIFs
Share GIFs from KLIPY in chat. Other ThereModernUI users see them pop up above their dock; everyone else sees a short [TMUI] [GIF] code.
Dock buttons
Choose what shows on the dock. It resizes itself to fit; hidden buttons stay in the Library.
Sync & data
Live sync
Inventory, clothing and item actions sync at startup and refresh automatically, so things work instantly and background changes (purchases, trades, gifts) appear on their own.
