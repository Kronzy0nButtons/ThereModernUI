Features

Everything in the dock

It takes over the compass slot and turns it into a proper dock. Pick which buttons you want, hide the rest in the Library.

Quick Bar, F1 to F12

Drop items, outfits and scenes on 12 slots and fire them with the F-keys, even while you're in the game.

WASD in Action Mode

Walk with WASD, jump with Space, reload with R. Hit Enter for Action Chat. Works however you switched Action Mode on.

Real clothing photos

The wardrobe shows actual catalog photos instead of grey boxes, cached on your PC so they load instantly.

Your own folders

Nested folders for items and every wardrobe category, drag and drop, export and import per avatar.

Emotes, routines, Shadow

Favorite emotes, build dance routines, or shadow a friend so their emotes play on you in sync.

Music + Party sync

Search and play music with playlists and a queue. Party sync plays along with other ModernUI users nearby.

GIFs in chat

Pick a GIF and it pops up for every ModernUI player around you. Everyone else just sees a short chat code.

Scenes

Chain an outfit, an emote, a song and a camera angle into one click, then put it on an F-key.

Clean notifications

The message bar becomes tidy toasts, and the annoying confirmations it can safely answer, it answers for you, like confirming Action Mode or the paintgun aim prompt.

Themes

Pick an accent colour for the whole UI, go compact, hide the compass or the Tbux balance.

Camera buttons

Standard view, face mirror and body mirror one click away.

Stays up to date

When a new version is out the dock tells you and links straight to the download.

Safe by design

What it does, and what it doesn't

It's a skin and a set of tools on top of your own client. No accounts, no logins, nothing running in the background.

  • Runs on your PC only. No servers of ours, no tracking, no background service.
  • Never asks for your password. It talks to the There client that's already running.
  • Backs up first. Every file it replaces is saved, and uninstalling puts the originals back.
  • Open source. Every line is on GitHub, and every installer has its SHA-256 listed.
  • Built on There Edge. The two client DLLs are rebuilt from hmphus's latest code with our small patches on top.
Install

Up and running in a minute

Download the setupGrab ThereModernUI-Setup.exe from the latest release.
Close There completelyThe installer swaps two of the client's DLLs, so the game can't be running.
Run it and pick your There folderThe one with There.exe in it. Common locations are found for you.
Start ThereThe dock sits where the compass used to be. Toggle it with Places > Compass.

On a Mac? A Mac version is coming soon.

Updating is the same: run the new setup over the top. Your folders, Quick Bar, scenes and settings stay put. To remove it, use Windows Settings > Apps > ThereModernUI > Uninstall.
